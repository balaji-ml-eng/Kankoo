Founder workflow
Open Settings > Money > Monthly books. Review the selected month's INR payments;
self-transfers and foreign-currency rows are excluded explicitly. Switch months,
search merchant/reference/note, or filter Unreviewed, All, Business, Personal or
Needs Evidence. Monthly results are not truncated to the latest 2,000 rows.
After importing a statement, Review monthly books opens its latest imported month.

Each row offers Personal, 100% Business or Split. The split sheet has 0/50/100
presets, a slider, exact percentage entry (two decimal places) and a business
purpose note. Percentages use integer basis points. Business paise are rounded
once, half up, without multiplying the full amount into an overflowing integer.
Net business outflow is reviewed business debits less reviewed business credits,
using each row's split; it is a cashflow measure, not a tax deduction or profit.

After reviewing a merchant, the inline Always use this split action saves an exact
normalized merchant rule. Rules also work with Unicode merchant names, normalize
case/common separators, and do not use substring/fuzzy matching. New debit payments
from CSV, SMS/email ingestion and manual entry can use the saved rule. Credits,
self-transfers, non-INR rows and already reviewed classifications are not changed.
Rules can be forgotten, or explicitly applied to existing unreviewed monthly rows.
Deleting a rule does not undo existing reviews. Classification and transaction
business-use fields update in the same transaction, preserving GST eligibility.

Evidence drawer
Tapping Match invoice evidence opens a focused bottom sheet. Suggestions are
deterministic: remaining amount, date proximity and vendor-token overlap, with
reasons and stable tie-breaking. Search by vendor or invoice number. Suggestions
never create an automatic link. Exact amounts can be linked with one tap; Change
amount supports partial allocations. One payment may cover several invoices and
several payments may settle one invoice. Adding to an existing allocation updates
it without creating duplicate rows. Unlinking and re-linking are audited.

Credits are explicitly allocated as refunds, against a paid invoice balance or
a credit note; they are not treated as supplier payments. Removing a payment that
still supports refunds is blocked until the refund links are removed. A credit's
general business classification can be recorded, but a receivables/income-invoice
workflow is not included in this expense-evidence milestone.

The link service re-reads balances inside a DB transaction, preventing stale
suggestions from over-allocating. It requires an INR business payment outside
self-transfers, confirmed bank details, consistent invoice arithmetic and an
authenticated original PDF. Missing/tampered PDF bytes prevent the link and keep
the existing ledger and file. Flagged bank rows require explicit owner confirmation.

Evidence Matched means allocations cover the full gross payment and the invoices
have consistent arithmetic. A 50% business share still requires evidence for the
full payment; the split is a separate fact. GST credit eligibility remains a
separate field, with no automatic upgrade from not assessed. No Tax Approved label.
The label describes the saved verified link; 1C export must re-verify attachments.

Invoice details
The invoice library lists the encrypted PDFs from 1A and lets the founder attach
another PDF, record its printed vendor/number/date/type and exact taxable value,
CGST, SGST, IGST, cess, rounding and total, then edit those details later. Optional
GSTIN inputs are checked for structure/checksum, not government registration.
Header tax components must sum exactly to the printed total. The PDF is
authenticated before saving. The same PDF cannot acquire a second invoice record.
Existing itemised invoices cannot have their headers silently changed while their
lines remain inconsistent; header editing is blocked for those records.
Recording manual details does not pretend OCR or CA review happened.

Integrity and existing app integration
Schema 6 changes only the business-use audit trigger; it creates no replacement
tables and performs no ledger reset. The upgrade is transactional and preserves
v5 IDs, amounts, notes, classifications, rules, links and previous audit events.
Classification/rule/allocation edits retain the append-only JSON audit triggers.
Rule-created business-use changes carry rule provenance rather than claiming a
human made them. Derived evidence statuses can be rebuilt from links and invoices.

Existing Activity delete/merge actions reject removal of a payment with evidence;
the UI explains how to unlink it in Monthly books. Transfer and currency changes
cannot silently hide a reviewed business payment. Merging away a reviewed row is
blocked; merge the unreviewed duplicate into the reviewed payment to keep its review.
Amount edits refresh evidence
status, while the DB guards prevent reducing amounts below existing allocations.
Edit failures keep the editor open with an actionable message. Automatic transfer
detection does not reclassify a payment already linked to invoice evidence.

Design and verification approach
Reused the 1A neutral light/dark financial theme, tabular figures and Indian paise
formatting. Selected chips and sliders use neutral ink rather than template blue.
Emerald is used for Evidence Matched; amber for pending review/evidence; crimson
for mismatches/errors. Compact summary, scrollable filters, lazy payment rows and
focused drawers preserve room for the actual review controls on a phone.
The workspace and drawer were rendered and inspected in light/dark phone layouts,
including 320px width with 150% text. PNGs: ../kankoo-m1b-previews/.

Remaining limits
No physical phone was connected for this run. Real Android picker, keyboard,
Keystore/lock and interruption behavior still need device pilot testing.
Invoice details are manual; OCR, an in-app PDF renderer and itemised line editing
were not added. General accounts receivable and ITC assessment are outside 1B.
PDF backup/portability remains blocked as documented in 1A; keep original PDFs.
1C CA ZIP/Excel export, file manifest generation and complete portability are next.
Milestone 2 durable native notification capture and subscription billing remain
outside this milestone. No public release or production-readiness claim is made.
