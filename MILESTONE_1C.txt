Kankoo - Milestone 1C: monthly CA export bundle
Date: 2026-11-26
Branch: feature/ca-evidence-ledger
Version: 1.1.0-dev.3+9; database schema 7

Scope
Monthly books > Export for CA opens the selected month's preview, save action
and generated-snapshot history. This work stays on the CA development branch;
main, the published APK and the separate iOS branch are not updated by it.

The ZIP is a readable CA handoff, not a complete restorable app/vault backup.
Ledger output is Excel-compatible UTF-8 CSV, not a native .xlsx workbook.
No OCR, GST/ITC approval, billing or Milestone 2 capture work is included.

Selection and amounts
The period is the device-local calendar month, start inclusive/end exclusive.
All month payments are exported, including personal, unreviewed, foreign-currency,
uncertain, possible-duplicate and self-transfer rows. Nothing is silently limited
to the latest 2,000 rows. Months over 50,000 payments fail without a receipt.
Currency flows are separate; no FX conversion or tax deduction is inferred.
Gross summary flows include all non-transfer rows and are provisional until
unreviewed/possible-duplicate rows are reconciled. Reviewed business totals also
exclude unconfirmed payments and possible duplicates. Paise/decimal formatting
uses integer arithmetic and aggregation uses BigInt, including beyond int64 sums.

Invoices include invoice dates within the month plus those linked to monthly
payments. All allocations of included invoices are retained. Out-of-month
payments needed for those allocations are placed in related_payments.csv and
must not be counted as this month's spending. Unassigned PDFs attached during
the month are included for review. Linked invoice header/line arithmetic is
revalidated; inconsistent links block export. Unlinked mismatches are labelled.
Business use and GST eligibility remain separate; Evidence Matched is not tax
approval. PDF metadata and manually entered invoice details remain reviewable.

Bundle format version 1
ledger.csv             All month payments, exact amounts and minor units,
                       classifications, business purpose, category/account,
                       bank status, possible duplicate ID, invoice/PDF links.
related_payments.csv   Out-of-month allocation parents, same ledger columns.
summary.csv            Per-currency gross and reviewed business flows.
invoices.csv           Header amounts, tax components, fresh arithmetic result,
                       GST review status and PDF path.
invoice_lines.csv      Stored line details and exact minor units, when present.
allocations.csv        Partial/combined payments, refunds and related-month flag.
documents.csv          Original filenames, exported paths, byte lengths, hashes.
source_events.csv     Source identities and hashes, not raw message payloads.
statement_imports.csv Reconciliation balances, row outcomes, parser/mapping,
                       source account, original statement name and hash.
audit.csv              Edits made in the month plus included-record history and
                       matching merchant-rule history; deleted-link snapshots
                       are retained even though no active link remains.
records.json           Canonical lossless transaction/invoice/document snapshots
                       with per-record SHA-256; matches export_items exactly.
invoices/*.pdf         Authenticated original PDF bytes, renamed using safe date,
                       vendor, number, document ID and hash; no path traversal.
README.txt             Selection, reconciliation, privacy and spreadsheet notes.
manifest.json          Run ID, timestamp, local period offsets and every other
                       file's relative path, byte length and SHA-256 digest.

CSV fields are quoted with doubled quotes, UTF-8 BOM and CRLF row endings.
Formula-like source text is prefixed with an apostrophe. Numeric amount strings
stay numeric; records.json preserves originals and integers beyond spreadsheet
precision. Import identifiers/references as text in Excel. The manifest excludes
itself; its SHA-256 is recorded in export_runs and shown/copyable in the app.
Hashes detect changes against a trusted digest; they do not prove authorship.

Integrity, persistence and save behavior
One DB transaction selects the snapshot, checks foreign keys/allocation limits,
authenticates and hashes every PDF, builds the ZIP and records the completed run
with its snapshots. A missing/tampered PDF, wrong key, inconsistent classification,
invalid linked invoice, size limit or database failure aborts without new history.
Original PDFs, encryption keys, ledger rows and prior audit/history are preserved.
The total uncompressed bundle, including manifests and records, is capped at
100 MiB. ZIP compression runs in a background isolate. Output is held in memory;
the feature creates no private plaintext ZIP cache or plaintext PDF copies.
The user-selected destination contains readable data; protect and share it safely.

Schema 7 adds UPDATE/DELETE guards to export_runs/export_items. Existing review
audit remains append-only. The v6 upgrade preserves ledger and export history.
Existing explicit ledger restore drops/reinstalls these guards atomically, and
its PDF-metadata-only backup rejection remains in force. Do not treat CA ZIPs as
a workaround for the still-pending complete encrypted-vault backup/restore.

Saving requires the app to be foregrounded and unlocked after generation.
Duplicate export taps are blocked during generation. A completed generation
records a receipt even if the destination picker is cancelled or saving fails;
history explicitly describes generation, not delivery or CA acceptance. A retry
generates a fresh snapshot. No retained ZIP or re-download is promised by history.

Remaining validation and limits
No physical phone/picker/CA spreadsheet pilot was performed. Real low-memory
phones, storage-full/interrupted saving, OEM lifecycle behavior, encrypted-vault
portability and release signing still require pilot validation. Export generation
holds a consistent DB transaction; large bundles consume memory and can delay
other writes. A generated receipt cannot attest that an external save completed.
This milestone does not resolve or republish the separate Android release's
startup failure investigation. No public release or production-readiness claim.
