Kankoo - distinctive visual refinement
2026-11-27 | feature/ca-evidence-ledger | 1.1.0-dev.6+12 | schema 8

Direction
Indigo and midnight with a restrained mint accent, a custom Kankoo mark,
and an evidence-led visual identity. This pass follows the request for a
more distinctive premium design after the initial interface polish.

Implemented
- Original vector K mark with a mint evidence dot. Drawn locally with no
  network fonts, downloaded artwork or additional package.
- Home has a compact financial summary and a mint classification ring.
  The ring reports actual reviewed/total INR rows, with a screen-reader
  description. Large text and narrow widths use a readable text summary.
- Tighter month navigation and task rows bring recent payments into the
  first screen at the 390x844 fixture size. Task icons and count badges
  provide distinct entry points for classification, evidence and bank checks.
- Books has a tonal monthly summary and separate payment cards. Existing
  classification, rules, splits and evidence actions are retained.
- Onboarding has original statement-to-evidence artwork and shorter copy.
  The artwork is decorative and contains no sample financial figures.
- Activity uses branded merchant initials for uncategorised transactions,
  keeping transfer/category icons where relevant. Foreign-amount warnings
  use contrasting amber text in both themes.
- Rounded action shapes align with the new mark and payment panels.
