Kankoo founder and CA interface polish
Date: 2026-11-27
Branch: feature/ca-evidence-ledger only
Version: 1.1.0-dev.5+11; database schema remains 8

Design
- Indigo #3347A8 actions, midnight #1B2855 to #294376 summary panel,
  cool #F7F8FC background, white panels and periwinkle selection states.
- Dark mode uses #0F1115 backgrounds and #B2C0FF primary controls.
- Emerald evidence, amber review and crimson mismatch statuses retain text
  labels. Badge text uses contrasting shades checked against both themes.
- One offline Roboto type system with tabular amounts, consistent spacing,
  bordered panels and prominent primary actions. No new dependency.

Workflow changes
- Home shows current business splits, classification progress and direct
  links to unreviewed payments, missing evidence and bank checks.
- Books is a primary tab. Existing Insights stays available in Settings.
- Home links update the month/filter even when Books was already open.
- Manual entry is available in Home and Activity headers without covering
  lower actions. Large INR amounts retain their exact paise and grouping.
- Onboarding starts with bank CSV; SMS remains optional with its existing
  permission, import, retry and skip behavior.
- Notification setup separates app selection, Android access and capture.
  The broad Android access disclosure stays visible before granting access.
- CA export puts counts and saving first, with scope and verification
  details expandable. The readable ZIP / incomplete-backup warning stays
  visible before saving. Cancel, failure and save-result behavior is kept.
- Books actions reflow at larger text sizes. Insights uses content-sized
  statistics and stacks utility links on narrow screens to prevent clipping.

Limits

acceptance still require a pilot. This UI pass does not establish a market
valuation or production readiness. Complete encrypted backup/restore,
subscriptions and signed production update testing remain separate work.
Main, iOS and startup-investigation branches are unchanged by this work.
